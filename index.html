How I work

Brief to craft

01

Discover

  1. 01

    Discover

    Listen to the brief, audience, and constraints. Surface what the brand, book, or product must say—and what it can leave unsaid.

  2. 02

    Design

    Explore directions, then refine. Prototypes and critiques lock the concept before pixels go final.

  3. 03

    Build

    High-fidelity layouts, artwork, UI, and production files. Specs and QA so print and digital match the intent.

  4. 04

    Hand off

    Style guides, templates, Figma libraries, and walkthroughs—so the team can extend the work without you in every file.
